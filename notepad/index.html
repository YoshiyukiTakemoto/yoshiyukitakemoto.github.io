Online notepad

A quick place to write that saves every keystroke in your browser. Keep several notes, search them, and come back any time. No account and nothing uploaded.

    How to use

    1. Start typing. Notes save automatically as you write.
    2. Press New note to add another. The first line becomes the title.
    3. Use the search box to find text in any note.
    4. Download a note as a .txt file to keep a backup or move it to another device.

    Where are my notes stored?

    In your browser’s local storage on this device. They stay after you close the tab or restart the computer. They are not synced to other devices and can be lost if you clear site data or use a private window, so download important notes.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can anyone else see my notes?

    No. Notes never leave your device. There is no server copy.

    Does it work offline?

    Yes. After your first visit the page loads without a connection, and you can add it to your home screen like an app.

    Is there a size limit?

    Browsers allow about 5 MB of local storage per site, which is roughly two million characters in total.
