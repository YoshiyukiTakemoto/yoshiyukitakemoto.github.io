How to write a Japanese era date

Japanese dates put the era first, then year, month and day: 令和7年10月6日 is Reiwa 7, 6 October, which is 6 October 2025. The first year of an era is written 元年 (gannen) instead of 1年. On forms, eras are often abbreviated to one letter: R (Reiwa), H (Heisei), S (Showa), T (Taisho) and M (Meiji).

Frequently asked questions

What year is Reiwa 1?

Reiwa 1 is 2019, starting from 1 May 2019. To convert a Reiwa year to a Gregorian year, add 2018.

How do I convert Heisei and Showa years?

Add 1988 to a Heisei year and 1925 to a Showa year. For example, Heisei 10 is 1998 and Showa 60 is 1985.

Why does 1989 have two eras?

Emperor Showa died on 7 January 1989. 1–7 January is Showa 64 and from 8 January it is Heisei 1. Likewise, 2019 is Heisei 31 until 30 April and Reiwa 1 from 1 May.

How are ages in the chart calculated?

The age is the age a person born in that year reaches on their birthday this year. Before their birthday they are one year younger.
