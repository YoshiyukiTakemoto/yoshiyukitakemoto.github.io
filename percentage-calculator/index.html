Percentage calculator

Six everyday percentage calculations on one page, each with the formula shown so you can check the working.

How to use

  1. Find the card that matches your question, such as “What is X% of Y?” or “Percentage change”.
  2. Replace the example numbers with your own. The answer updates as you type.
  3. Check the formula under the answer if you need to show your working.

Percentage formulas

  • X% of Y = Y × X ÷ 100
  • X as a percent of Y = X ÷ Y × 100
  • Percentage change = (new − old) ÷ old × 100
  • Price after X% off = price × (1 − X ÷ 100)
  • Whole from a part = part ÷ X × 100

Percent vs percentage points

If an interest rate rises from 2% to 3%, it has gone up by 1 percentage point, but by 50 percent. Use the percentage change card for the second figure.

Frequently asked questions

Why doesn’t a 20% rise followed by a 20% fall return to the start?

The fall is 20% of the larger number. 100 → 120 → 96. To get back from 120 to 100 you need a fall of about 16.7%.

How do I work out a percentage of a total?

Use “X is what percent of Y?”. For example, 45 out of 60 is 75%.

How do I find the price before a discount?

Use “Find the whole from a part”. If you paid 70 after 30% off, 70 is 70% of the original price: 70 ÷ 70 × 100 = 100.
