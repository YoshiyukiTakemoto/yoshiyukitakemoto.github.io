Kit Jump: endless platformer

Run and jump through a world that builds itself as you go. Stomp enemies, grab coins and see how far you can get. No download, no sign-up.

How to use

  1. Press Space or tap the game to start.
  2. Move with the arrow keys or A / D. Jump with Space, ↑ or W. Hold jump to go higher.
  3. Jump on enemies to defeat them. Touching them from the side ends the run, and so does falling into a gap.
  4. On phones, use the on-screen buttons. Press ⛶ for full screen and P to pause.

Scoring

  • 1 point for every block of distance
  • 10 points per coin
  • 50 points per enemy stomped

The level gets harder the further you go: wider gaps and more enemies. Your best score is saved in this browser.

Frequently asked questions

Does the level ever end?

No. The world is generated as you run, so every run is different.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. On-screen buttons appear on touch screens. Turn your phone sideways and use full screen for the best view.

Is it free?

Yes, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no sign-up.
