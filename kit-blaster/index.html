Kit Blaster: retro space shooter

A fast vertical shooter in the arcade tradition. Your ship fires automatically, so focus on dodging. Collect power-ups, build combos and see how many waves you can survive.

How to use

  1. Press Space or tap the screen to start.
  2. Move with the arrow keys or WASD. Your ship fires on its own. Hold Shift to move slowly through tight gaps; a white dot shows your hit area.
  3. On phones, drag anywhere on the screen. The ship moves with your finger but stays above it, so you can see it.
  4. Grab P to power up your shots and ♥ for an extra life. A boss appears every fifth wave.

Scoring and combos

Each enemy has a point value. Destroy enemies within about a second of each other to raise the combo multiplier, up to ×8. Getting hit resets the combo and lowers your shot power by one level. Power-ups collected at full power are worth 1,000 points.

Enemies

  • Drones (pink squares) sweep down in lines.
  • Divers (orange arrows) pause, then dash straight at you.
  • Turrets (teal hexagons) stop and fire aimed shots. They take several hits.
  • Spinners (yellow rings) loop in from the sides.
  • Bosses change attack patterns as their health drops.

Frequently asked questions

Is my high score saved?

Yes, in this browser. Share your score with the buttons that appear after a game.

Can I turn off the sound?

Yes. Use the speaker button in the top-right corner of the game.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. It is designed for portrait screens. Use full screen (⛶) for the best view.
