BMI calculator

Work out your body mass index from height and weight, see where it falls on the WHO adult scale and find the weight range considered healthy for your height.

BMI

How to use

  1. Choose cm / kg or ft·in / lb.
  2. Enter your height and weight.
  3. Read your BMI, its category and the healthy weight range for your height.

BMI categories (WHO, adults)

BMICategory
Below 18.5Underweight

25.0–29.9Overweight

35.0–39.9Obesity class II
40 and aboveObesity class III

How BMI is calculated

BMI = weight in kilograms ÷ (height in metres)². For example, 65 kg at 1.70 m gives 65 ÷ 2.89 = 22.5. In imperial units: 703 × weight in pounds ÷ (height in inches)².

Frequently asked questions

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

No. It doesn’t distinguish muscle from fat, so athletes may score high. It is a quick screening measure, not a diagnosis.

Does BMI work for children?

Not with these categories. Children and teenagers are assessed with BMI-for-age percentiles that depend on age and sex.

Why do some countries use different cut-offs?

Studies show higher health risks at lower BMI in some Asian populations, so Japan, for example, classes a BMI of 25 or more as obesity.
