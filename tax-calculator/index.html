VAT and sales tax calculator

Add VAT or sales tax to a price, or work out the price before tax from a total. Choose a preset rate or enter your own.

How to use

  1. Choose Add tax if your price excludes tax, or Remove tax if it already includes it.
  2. Enter the amount and pick a tax rate, or choose Other to type any rate.
  3. Read the net price, the tax and the gross price.

How to remove VAT correctly

A common mistake is to take 20% off a VAT-inclusive price. That gives the wrong answer, because the tax was 20% of the net price. Divide by 1.2 instead: £120 ÷ 1.2 = £100 net, so the VAT is £20, not £24.

Formulas

  • Gross = net × (1 + rate ÷ 100)
  • Net = gross ÷ (1 + rate ÷ 100)
  • Tax = gross − net

Frequently asked questions

What is the UK VAT rate?

The standard rate is 20%. A reduced rate of 5% and a zero rate apply to some goods and services.

Can I use this for US sales tax?

Yes. Choose Other and enter your combined state and local rate, such as 8.875% for New York City.

Why does rounding matter?

Tax is often a fraction of a cent. Most invoices round to the nearest cent, but some systems always round down. Pick the method your invoices use.
